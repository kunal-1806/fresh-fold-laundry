Questions

Good to know

How long does an order take?

Standard orders are returned within 24 hours. Express orders are back the same day, within 6 hours of pickup.

What if a garment gets damaged?

We photograph every item at intake. If something goes wrong, we reimburse up to 10× the cleaning cost — no arguments.

Do you use harsh chemicals?

No. We use biodegradable, dermatologist-tested detergents, and a separate fragrance-free wash is available on request.

Which areas do you cover?

Pickup and delivery are free within 5 km of our hubs. Beyond that, a small ₹49 distance fee applies.

How do I pay?

Pay after delivery by UPI, card or cash. Monthly plans are billed at the start of the cycle.
